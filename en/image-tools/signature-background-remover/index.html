    Start with the paper, light and ink

    Photograph the signature straight on, in even light, with the page flat. Dark ink on plain white or cream paper gives this tool a clearer separation between the writing and background. Crop away unrelated text before you begin using Crop photo.

    The tool estimates the brightness of nearby paper before separating the ink, which helps with uneven lighting. Strong shadow edges, printed lines and other writing can still remain. Crop these out or use the eraser, then inspect the PNG on a dark preview background. Signatures over photographs or patterned backgrounds need a different workflow.

    Preserve thin strokes without keeping a grey rectangle

    Increase ink sensitivity if pale strokes disappear. Lower it when too much paper or shadow remains. Edge softness controls the transition from ink to transparency, so inspect small loops and pen lifts rather than checking only the overall shape.

    Use the eraser on a stray dot or line in the result preview. Undo restores the last eraser stroke. Changing sensitivity or colour regenerates the image from the original, so make those adjustments before erasing. Auto-trim removes empty space; turn it off to preserve the original canvas dimensions.

    Download a transparent PNG for your document

    A checkerboard is a preview of transparency, not part of the file. Switch to white, cream or dark to inspect the result against a different background. The download remains a PNG with transparency, whichever preview background you choose.

    PNG retains transparent pixels; JPG cannot. Insert the downloaded PNG into your document as an image. This tool prepares an image of an existing signature; it does not create a cryptographic digital signature, prove identity or sign a PDF.

    Questions, answered

    Is my signature uploaded?

    No. Image processing takes place in this browser. Toolmova uses Google Analytics for website usage; the tool does not send image contents or filenames to Analytics.

    Why did part of my signature disappear?

    The writing may be too close in brightness to the paper. Increase ink sensitivity or take a clearer photo with darker ink and more even light.

    Why is my downloaded image showing a white background?

    Some image viewers display transparency on white. Insert the PNG over a coloured background to check it. Avoid converting it to JPG if transparency matters.
