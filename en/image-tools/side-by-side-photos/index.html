    A straightforward way to put pictures together

    Use a horizontal pair for a before-and-after comparison, two product angles or a photo with a reference image. Use the vertical layout for a sequence of screenshots or a short visual walkthrough.

    The order in the image list is the order in the download. Use the move buttons to switch the left and right images. This works with a keyboard as well as on a touch screen; dragging is not required.

    Keep the whole picture, without stretching it

    Horizontal layouts match image heights; vertical layouts match image widths. Each source keeps its aspect ratio and is fitted into the shared layout without cropping. Different proportions can therefore produce different tile widths or heights.

    For example, two 800 × 600 photos placed side by side at a total width of 1600 pixels produce a 1600 × 600 result when spacing and margins are zero. If you need matching crops instead, use Crop photo before joining the pictures.

    Set the size for the place you will use it

    Output width describes the entire finished canvas, including margins. Spacing and margins are measured in output pixels. Choose a width that leaves text readable; a long screenshot compressed into a small canvas will be harder to read.

    PNG is a useful choice for diagrams and text; JPG is often smaller for photos. The selected background fills gaps and transparent source areas. This tool joins images into one picture rather than creating a PDF or blending subjects with AI.

    Questions, answered

    Can I join more than two photos?

    Yes. Add up to eight images with a combined decoded area of 24 megapixels. Large output dimensions may require a smaller output width.

    Do I need to install an app?

    No. The merger runs in this browser. The image contents are not sent to a conversion server.

    Will it crop or blend my photos?

    No. It fits the complete images proportionally into a row or column. Crop the source first if you want a different composition.
